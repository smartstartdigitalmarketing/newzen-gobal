Engineering and Execution for Industrial Electrical Infrastructure

We provide MEP and electrical engineering services for industrial facilities, manufacturing plants, warehouses and infrastructure projects.

Our support can extend from electrical planning and engineering through installation, testing and commissioning, helping customers establish reliable electrical infrastructure for their operations.

Services can cover power distribution, equipment electrification, lighting, cabling, cable management, earthing and associated electrical works.

Our Services

  • Electrical Engineering: Electrical system planning, load assessment, distribution architecture and equipment selection.
  • Industrial Electrification: Electrical infrastructure for machines, production lines, utilities and plant facilities.
  • Cabling & Cable Management: Power, control and instrumentation cabling, cable trays, routing, termination and associated works.
  • Lighting & Facility Electrical Systems: Industrial lighting, distribution and associated electrical systems.
  • Installation & Commissioning: Site installation, testing, energization and commissioning support.

Your Value

Coordinated engineering Reliable execution Reduced project interfaces Safe installation Schedule-focused delivery

One Integration Partner Across Multiple Systems

Complex industrial projects involve multiple equipment suppliers, technologies and engineering disciplines. The challenge is not simply implementing each individual system — it is ensuring that all systems work together as one integrated operation.

As a Master Systems Integrator, we can provide engineering and integration leadership across electrical, control, automation, instrumentation, communication and digital systems.

We work with customer teams, OEMs, EPC contractors and technology suppliers to define interfaces, coordinate engineering and integrate individual systems into the overall plant architecture.

Our Services

  • System Architecture & Engineering: Develop integrated electrical, control, automation and communication architectures.
  • Interface Engineering: Define and coordinate interfaces between equipment, systems and technology suppliers.
  • Multi-Vendor Integration: Integrate products and systems from multiple manufacturers into a coordinated solution.
  • Project Engineering & Coordination: Coordinate engineering activities, documentation and system dependencies.
  • System Integration & Testing: Integration testing, FAT/SAT support and validation of system functionality.
  • Site Implementation & Commissioning: Coordinate site integration and bring individual systems together into operation.

Your Value

Single-point integration responsibility Fewer interface gaps Reduced technical risk Better project coordination Faster commissioning

From Installed Systems to Stable Production

Completing installation is only one milestone. A plant, production line or machine must be safely commissioned, integrated and stabilized before it can achieve its intended operating performance.

We support industrial customers during the critical transition from project completion to operational production.

Our engineers work alongside project, OEM, operations and maintenance teams to identify issues, validate system functionality and support production ramp-up.

Our Services

  • Pre-Commissioning Checks: Verification of electrical, control, instrumentation and communication systems before start-up.
  • System Commissioning: Functional testing and commissioning of electrical and automation systems.
  • I/O & Sequence Validation: Verification of field signals, control sequences, interlocks and operating logic.
  • Integrated System Testing: Validate communication and operation across interconnected machines and systems.
  • Production Start-up Support: Technical support during initial production and equipment ramp-up.
  • System Optimization: Fine-tuning of control parameters and operating sequences following commissioning.

Your Value

Faster start-up Reduced commissioning issues Better system coordination Shorter path to stable production Engineering support when it matters most

Protect the Systems That Keep Your Plant Running

Control and automation systems are critical to plant availability. As systems age, customers face increasing challenges from component failures, obsolete hardware, unsupported software and loss of engineering knowledge.

Our lifecycle services help maintain the reliability, availability and maintainability of industrial control systems.

We support both day-to-day maintenance requirements and longer-term modernization planning.

Our Services

  • Preventive Maintenance: Periodic inspection and health checks of control and automation systems.
  • Breakdown Support: Troubleshooting and engineering support for control-system failures.
  • PLC, HMI & SCADA Support: Diagnostics, software support, backups, modifications and system troubleshooting.
  • System Backup & Documentation: Support for maintaining current programs, configuration backups and technical documentation.
  • Obsolescence Management: Identify aging or discontinued equipment and develop migration or replacement strategies.
  • Control System Modernization: Upgrade legacy PLC, HMI, SCADA, drives and associated automation infrastructure.

Your Value

Improve system availability Reduce unplanned downtime Protect engineering knowledge Manage obsolescence Extend asset life

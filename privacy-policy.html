Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 24, 2026 • Newzen Global Solutions Compliance Policy

1. Information Collection & Scope

At Newzen Global Solutions, we prioritize the protection and confidentiality of your commercial and personal data. As a B2B industrial automation channel partner and system integrator, we collect technical requirements, corporate contact details (such as names, business emails, phone numbers, and company affiliations), and site specifications when you request quotes, consult with our application engineers, or engage our technical support team.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is strictly utilized to process your engineering inquiries, deliver custom control panel specifications, manage system integration contracts, and maintain asset health under AMC agreements. We do not sell, rent, or lease client information to third parties. Data may be shared with authorized original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) strictly when necessary for component warranty registration or technical validation.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses functional and analytical cookies to enhance your browsing experience, optimize site performance, and analyze traffic patterns across our solutions and technical resources. Cookies do not store sensitive payment information or proprietary manufacturing blueprints. You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

4. Data Security & ISO Compliance

We maintain robust physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards in accordance with ISO 9001 compliance standards. Proprietary system schematics, industrial network configurations, and SCADA data pipelines transmitted to Newzen Global are stored in encrypted cloud environments with strict role-based access control.

Electrical Components

Overview & Project Scope

Electrical components form the foundation of every industrial installation. From incoming power and distribution to protection, switching and machine-level electrical systems, selecting components with the correct ratings and specifications is essential for reliable operation.

We support requirements across switchgear, circuit protection, contactors, relays, power supplies, transformers, terminal systems, enclosures, panel accessories and related electrical products. Share your part number, electrical ratings, drawings, BOM or application requirements, and we can help identify a suitable product for your system.

Typical Applications:

Power distribution Control panels Machine electrical systems Motor circuits Protection & switching Industrial power supplies

Electrical Infrastructure & Accessories

Overview & Project Scope

A dependable electrical installation requires more than primary electrical equipment. The correct cables, routing systems, termination products and installation accessories are essential for ensuring safe, organized and reliable power and signal distribution throughout machines, plants and industrial facilities.

We support a broad range of power and control cables, instrumentation cables, communication cables, cable trays, cable ladders, conduits, cable glands, connectors, terminals, lugs, junction boxes and associated installation accessories. Whether your requirement is based on an exact part number, cable specification, project BOM, drawing or application, we can help identify suitable products based on electrical ratings, environmental conditions, installation requirements and applicable industrial standards.

Typical Applications:

Power & control cabling Instrumentation wiring Cable routing & management Machine wiring Control panels Plant electrical installations Industrial connectivity Project infrastructure

Control Components

Overview & Project Scope

From a simple machine control function to a complete automated production process, the correct control architecture is critical to performance, reliability and maintainability.

We support a wide range of PLCs, programmable relays, HMIs, controllers, I/O systems, drives, soft starters, motion control products, safety controllers and associated automation components. Whether you are replacing an existing component or developing a new machine or process, provide us with your part number, I/O requirement, control philosophy or application specification and we can help you select the appropriate solution.

Typical Applications:

Machine automation Process control Motor & drive control Motion applications Production lines Control system upgrades

Field elements

Overview & Project Scope

Field instruments and devices provide the information and physical interaction required to monitor and control industrial processes. Correct selection depends on factors such as measurement range, process conditions, material, accuracy, environment, communication and installation requirements.

We support sensors, transmitters, switches, encoders, measurement devices, valves, actuators and other field-level components for industrial applications. Tell us what you need to detect, measure, monitor or control, along with the operating conditions, and we can assist in identifying a suitable product.

Typical Applications:

Temperature Pressure Flow Level Position Proximity Speed Process measurement Machine sensing & actuation

Digital elements

Overview & Project Scope

Modern industrial systems generate valuable operational data. Digital elements help transform this information into actionable insights for production teams, maintenance personnel and management.

We support technologies for industrial data acquisition, monitoring, visualization, edge computing, gateways, industrial PCs, remote monitoring and IIoT applications. From connecting an existing machine to a digital platform to developing a new data collection architecture, we can help identify the hardware required to bridge the gap between the shop floor and digital systems.

Typical Applications:

Machine data acquisition Production monitoring Edge computing Remote monitoring IIoT Condition monitoring Digitalization

Industrial software

Overview & Project Scope

Industrial software provides the interface between equipment, operators, engineers and business systems. Selecting the right software platform requires consideration of system architecture, communication protocols, scalability, licensing and integration requirements.

We support requirements across SCADA, HMI software, engineering and programming tools, historian solutions, visualization, reporting, industrial data platforms and related software technologies. Whether you need a specific software license or are evaluating a platform for an application, we can help identify the appropriate solution based on your system requirements and existing automation environment.

Typical Applications:

SCADA Visualization Engineering Data logging Historian Reporting Production monitoring Industrial analytics

Industrial Network & infrastructure

Overview & Project Scope

Industrial networks operate in demanding environments where reliability, availability and secure communication are critical. The right network infrastructure ensures that machines, controllers, field devices and information systems communicate consistently.

We support industrial Ethernet switches, routers, gateways, wireless solutions, protocol converters, communication modules, network accessories and associated industrial infrastructure. Share your network architecture, communication protocol, number of devices, environmental requirements or existing equipment, and we can help identify suitable networking products.

Typical Applications:

Plant networks Machine communication Fieldbus integration Wireless networks Secure remote access Industrial IoT connectivity

Automation modules & Systems

Overview & Project Scope

Modern manufacturing and material-handling applications often require more than individual automation components. They require purpose-built functional modules that combine mechanical equipment, sensors, drives, controls and automation to perform a specific operation reliably and efficiently.

We support a range of standard and bespoke automation modules and systems including weighing and dispensing systems, conveyors, lifters, feeders, industrial weighing scales, AGVs/AMRs, MiR autonomous mobile robots, cobots and other application-specific automation units. Whether you need an individual automation module for integration into an existing line or a purpose-built system for a new application, we can help identify, configure and supply the appropriate solution based on material characteristics, load, capacity, throughput, movement, accuracy, operating environment and integration requirements.

Typical Applications:

Material conveying Weighing & dispensing Feeding & dosing Lifting & transfer Line-side material movement Autonomous material transport Collaborative automation Machine tending Production handling Factory automation

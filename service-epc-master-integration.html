EPC PROJECT LIFECYCLE

Step 01

DESIGN

Conceptual mapping, Front-End Engineering Design (FEED), and architectural topology development ensuring compliance with global standards.

Step 02

PROCURE

Vendor-neutral selection and strict supply chain procurement of all PLCs, VFDs, Instrumentation, and mechanical field devices.

Step 03

INTEGRATE

In-house manufacturing of control panels coupled with rigorous SCADA/PLC software programming and Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT).

Step 04

COMMISSION & SUPPORT

Seamless on-site installation, loop checks, Site Acceptance Testing (SAT), and final handover, backed by 24/7 maintenance.

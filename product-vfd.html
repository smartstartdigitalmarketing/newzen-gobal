Variable Frequency Drives (VFD)

Precision AC motor speed & torque controllers.

Product Overview

Engineered for maximum energy efficiency, Newzen Global Variable Frequency Drives (VFD) provide precise speed and torque control for heavy pumps, blowers, and conveyor systems. Built-in active front-end technology suppresses line harmonics while integrated Safe Torque Off (STO) enhances worker protection.

Key Specifications & Features

  • 🌀Low Harmonic Active Front-End (IEEE 519 Compliant)
  • 🌀Integrated Safe Torque Off (STO) Safety Function
  • 🌀Sensorless Vector & Closed-Loop Encoder Control
  • 🌀Up to 30% Energy Savings for HVAC & Fluid Handling

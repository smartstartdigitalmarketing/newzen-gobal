Integrated Electrical Systems

Overview & Project Scope

We design and deliver integrated electrical systems for industrial facilities, machines and infrastructure projects. Our approach considers the complete electrical requirement - from power distribution and protection to motor control, lighting and integration with automation systems. We support projects from load assessment, system design and equipment selection through panel engineering, manufacturing, testing, installation and commissioning.

Our Core Capabilities:

  • Electrical Design & Architecture: Comprehensive load assessment, system sizing & single line diagrams.
  • Panel Engineering & Manufacturing: Custom LT distribution, PCC, MCC, and drive panel enclosures.
  • System Integration & FAT/SAT Testing: Factory acceptance testing and full automation system integration.
  • Installation & Commissioning: On-site cabling, loop testing, bus duct routing, and 24/7 handover support.

Our Electrical Panel Range

LT Electrical Panels

Engineering and supply of low-voltage distribution and control panels designed around project loads, protection requirements, operating philosophy and applicable standards.

Applications:

Main distribution • Sub-distribution • PCC • MCC • Power distribution • Machine electrical systems

Lighting Panels

Engineered lighting distribution and control panels for industrial facilities, warehouses, utilities and infrastructure.

Applications:

Industrial lighting • Facility lighting • Warehouse lighting • Outdoor lighting • Automated lighting control

VFD & Drive Panels

Engineered drive panels for precise and energy-efficient motor control, integrating variable-frequency drives, protection, control and communication systems.

Applications:

Pumps • Fans • Conveyors • Process equipment • Material handling • Machine applications

Integrated Visualization & Data Collection Systems

Turning Plant Data into Operational Visibility

Industrial facilities generate valuable information across machines, processes, utilities and production systems. We integrate these individual data sources into platforms that provide meaningful operational visibility.

Our solutions connect shop-floor equipment, PLCs, instruments and industrial networks with visualization and data-collection systems.

Depending on the application, solutions can provide dashboards, production information, alarms, trends, historical records, equipment status, utility data and operational reporting.

Applications

Production monitoring • Machine data collection • Energy monitoring • Utility monitoring • OEE inputs • Condition monitoring • Traceability • Management dashboards

Integrated Raw Material Feeding & Dosing Systems

Engineered Systems for Feeding, Weighing & Dispensing

We design and deliver integrated raw material feeding, weighing, dosing and dispensing systems for bulk solids, liquids and small ingredients, engineered around your material characteristics, process requirements, capacity and accuracy.

Our solutions combine mechanical handling and feeding equipment, weighing systems, electrical and instrumentation, control automation and recipe-management software into one coordinated system.

From concept and engineering through manufacturing, integration, testing, installation and commissioning, we provide complete system responsibility for reliable, repeatable and traceable material preparation.

Typical Applications

Bulk solid feeding • Liquid dosing • Small ingredient weighing & dispensing • Automated batching • Material transfer • Recipe-controlled dispensing • Formulation management

Integrated Intralogistics Automation Systems

Automating Material Movement Within Your Facility

Efficient internal material movement is increasingly important for modern manufacturing and warehousing operations.

We develop integrated intralogistics automation solutions that coordinate material movement between receiving, storage, production, assembly, inspection, packing and dispatch operations.

Our role as a systems integrator is to bring together the required automation, controls, sensors, identification technologies, material-handling equipment, software and communication systems into a coordinated solution.

Applications

Automated material movement • Conveyor systems • Production logistics • Warehouse automation • Line feeding • Sorting • Routing • Tracking

Integrated Inspection Systems

Automated Inspection for Quality, Accuracy and Traceability

Quality inspection increasingly requires automated systems capable of making consistent decisions at production speed.

We integrate inspection solutions combining sensors, machine vision, identification technologies, measurement devices, controls, automation and data systems according to the application.

Solutions can be designed to detect product presence, dimensions, check weights, orientation, assembly conditions, defects, identification information and other defined quality parameters.

Inspection results can also be integrated with production systems for rejection, sorting, traceability, reporting and quality-data collection.

Applications

Presence/absence inspection • Dimensional checks • Assembly verification • Vision inspection • Barcode/QR identification • Defect detection • Sorting & rejection • Traceability
